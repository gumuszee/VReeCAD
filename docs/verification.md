## Remaining in-headset checks (owner)

Start the viewer (workbench BlocksCAD, "Open XR viewer"), wear the headset until the session is `FOCUSED`, then check:

1. Move tool (free and locked): choose Move, touch a box made with Create at a corner, an edge, a face and inside it (each highlights), tap to select, drag it (grid steps, turn with the wrist), scale it with the trigger of the other hand, hold A during the grab (padlock closes, grid and angle lock), cancel with a grip, undo. Check that the highlight is steady and that the quick radial stays closed during a grab. Check that the reach and the highlights are easy to judge, that the highlight does not flicker (also with the selected element under the hand) and that selecting also shows on the desktop. Push the stick for the option discs of Create and Move.
2. Create tool: choose Create on the palette, move the tip (a cross shows the grid point), drag with the trigger and release; try every shape on the stick left and right, the grid on the stick up and down, a tap, a grip during a drag and undo. Check that the snapped point and the box feel right at different world scales.
3. Quick radial: hold `button1` of the right controller (A on the Steam Frame), move the hand towards an item, release; check that it is not triggered while a grip holds the world and that the dead zone and the ring size feel right.
4. Faces: tap a face (it runs on release), hold a face for 0.4 s and drop it on another slot, on a filled slot (swap) and on the "Hide face" side; use Show hidden and Reset layout.
5. `button2` of the right controller (B on the Steam Frame): a tap undoes, a hold of 0.4 s redoes, with toasts; while a grip holds the world it is still the 1 : 1 lock. Toast size and place, tooltips after 0.5 s on a face.
6. Pressing the left D-pad left undoes and D-pad right redoes the last document change, with a haptic tick (the Edit faces do the same).
7. After a two-grip gesture, releasing one grip first and moving the other hand does not move the model; after releasing both, a one-grip drag works again.
8. The haptic ticks are noticeable and not annoying: hover over a face, activation, slider steps, grip start, scale doubling, reset.
9. Value panels: select a box with the Select tool or the Move tool (the left hand ticks and the carousel turns to the new side), look at the left controller (Value on top, then Number pad, Keys, Variables), press Next value until Width is picked (the readout and the Value n of m cell follow), move the tip close to the value slider without touching it, push the stick up and down, drag the value slider by its bar, drag the step slider to 0.1 mm and push the stick (the value moves by 0.1 mm), type `2*(3 cm)` on the number pad (the readout turns orange and shows it) and press Enter, then select another box and grab it, then clear the selection (the panels go), undo a few times. Check that the panels appear without any extra input, that coming close focuses the value slider without any press, that pressing beside the bar does nothing and the bar is easy to hit (also on the Session sliders, Angle and Length), that the stick goes to the tool when no slider is focused, that the stacked panels are in reach and readable with the tip and the laser, that nothing in the panels floats over the model or the controller, and that the repeat speed of the stick and the slider ranges feel right.
10. Carousel size and colour: the panels are compact enough for all the tools, each panel has its own tint and the text stays legible at arm's length; the frame rate does not drop while the carousel is shown, with the stick turning it and with a face hovered.
11. Select tool: choose it from the palette, touch a box and press the trigger (the whole object is selected and the value panels appear); push the stick towards Add and select a second object, push towards Remove and take one out, push the same section again (back to Replace); switch the target to Face, Edge and Vertex and select several of each; check that the highlight shows what a press selects and that the reach of each target is easy to hit.
12. Quick radial hub: with the Move tool active, press and release A without moving the hand (the Select tool is chosen, with a tick); hold A and move out to a segment (the hub goes dark and the segment is chosen); check that a trembling hand stays inside the dead zone.
13. Modify panel: select two overlapping boxes with the Select tool (Add), press Fuse, Cut or Common on the Modify panel, then change a size with a value slider on the source and watch the result follow; copy an object and a fused object; undo each. Check that the panel is easy to find (it has its own side, violet) and that the order of the selection (the first object is the base of a cut) is clear from the toasts. Mirror and Pattern an object along each axis, press Colour a few times.
14. Push/Pull tool (a box, a cylinder and an extruded sketch change their sizes, other solids get a fuse or cut feature; select the box and look at the Value panel to see Height change): choose it, touch a face of a box (only faces light up), press the trigger on it and move the hand away from the box (the ghost grows, the readout names the push), release, then pull a face into the box, tap a face, cancel with a grip, undo. Check that the distance along the face normal is easy to judge and that the grid steps feel right.
15. Variables: select a box, tip on the Length slider, press Make variable on the Variables panel (a toast names the variable), pick Width, press the variable face (its name appears on the Number pad), type `* 2 + 10 mm` and Enter, then push the stick on the Length slider and watch Width follow; try the stick on Width (refused), Unbind, undo. Check that the four stacked panels are reachable and readable, that the long slider text does not run out of its row, and that the variable faces are easy to tell apart.
16. Points tool: make a Block with the Create tool (the second page of shapes), choose Points (every corner is marked), move the tip to a corner, press the trigger and drag it (the solid follows, grid steps, A held leaves the grid), release, undo; switch to Split on the stick, press on an edge, drag the new point out; move a corner so far that the block would cross itself and check that the point stops. Check that the markers are easy to see and that picking a point or an edge is easy. Push the stick towards Flat faces, press on a corner of a box-shaped block and try to drag it (it stays, the readout says flat), split an edge and drag the new point (it slides along the edge), then switch to Free faces and drag a corner (the faces bend into triangles). Check that the two kinds of faces are clear from the disc and the readout.
17.
